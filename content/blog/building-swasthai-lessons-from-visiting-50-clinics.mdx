When I started SwasthAI, I assumed the core challenge was Electronic Health Records (EHR). But after spending weeks observing daily OPD operations in Lucknow and surrounding districts, I realized the real bottleneck occurred before the doctor's door ever opened.

<Callout type="tip" title="Field Discovery">
If software takes more than 5 seconds for a receptionist to interact with under morning rush pressure, it gets abandoned instantly.
</Callout>

## Key Takeaways from 50+ Clinic Visits

1. **Pass the Monday 9:30 AM Test**: Software must handle rapid multi-patient check-ins without slowing down reception.
2. **Respect the Doctor's Flow**: Clinicians do not want multi-click software during consultation. They want actionable clinical context in 3 seconds.
3. **Simplicity Demands Iteration**: Reducing our patient intake form from 12 fields to 5 core fields increased completion rates from 40% to 87%.

> "Building healthcare software requires deep empathy for the front-desk staff who manage the human chaos of OPD queues every single day."
