Not all OPD queues operate under identical rules. A pediatric clinic manages completely different urgency signals than an orthopedic practice.

<Callout type="info" title="Specialty Alignment">
Generic intake tools fail because pain severity in orthopedics requires different weighting than fever in infants under 12 months.
</Callout>

## Pediatric Triage Needs

In pediatrics:
- Infants under 12 months with high fever or respiratory distress require immediate escalation.
- Parents fill out intake forms on behalf of children, requiring clear, plain-language questions.

## Orthopedic Triage Needs

In orthopedics:
- Pain is universal, rendering standard 1-10 pain scales ineffective.
- Urgency scoring weights recent trauma, inability to bear weight, and acute onset versus chronic complaints.
