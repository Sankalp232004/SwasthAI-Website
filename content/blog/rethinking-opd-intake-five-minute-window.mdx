In a typical outpatient clinic, the most chaotic minutes occur before the patient ever enters the consultation room. Patients arrive in waves, registration registers fill up, and receptionists make high-pressure decisions based solely on arrival sequence or visual impression.

<Callout type="info" title="Key Clinical Insight">
Traditional first-come, first-served queues treat all medical complaints equally — regardless of whether a patient has a mild cough or escalating acute abdominal pain.
</Callout>

## The Hidden Cost of First-Come, First-Served Queues

Traditional first-come, first-served queues create two major failure points in daily OPD operations:

1. **Clinical Risk**: Patients with escalating symptoms (like high fever or chest discomfort) wait behind routine checkups.
2. **Consultation Friction**: Doctors spend the first 2-3 minutes of every consultation gathering basic administrative data that could have been collected at reception.

### Why Time is Wasted at Reception

When front-desk staff rely on manual paper ledgers:
- Patient details are transcribed slowly by hand.
- Peak morning rushes overwhelm reception staff within 15 minutes.
- Priority decisions are made arbitrarily without standardized clinical triage guidelines.

> "A child with a fever of 104°F should never wait behind an elderly patient who came 30 minutes early for a routine blood pressure checkup simply because of arrival order."

## Structured Intake: The 90-Second Solution

By introducing a guided digital intake flow at reception — accessible via QR code on any smartphone — clinics capture chief complaints, age, severity, and basic vitals in under 90 seconds.

```json
{
  "triage_inputs": {
    "chief_complaint": "Acute fever & chest discomfort",
    "duration_hours": 4,

    "category": "HIGH_PRIORITY"
  }
}
```

### The Three Operational Transformations

- **Pre-Consultation Context**: The doctor receives a structured summary before the patient enters.
- **Fair Waiting Room Management**: Transparent queue status eliminates receptionist arguments.
- **Enhanced Patient Retention**: Clear communication reduces waiting room anxiety by up to 60%.
