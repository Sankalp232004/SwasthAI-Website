Clinical Priority System

Four levels. Instant assignment.

Doctor retains full override control at all times.

🚨 Emergency

Immediate Action

Chest pain, severe breathing difficulty, altered consciousness.

Seen first. No exceptions.
🔴 Red

Urgent

Severe acute pain, critical vitals, high fever with distress.

Elevated immediately on check-in.
🟡 Amber

Semi-Urgent

Moderate symptoms requiring timely attention.

Managed above routine flow.
🟢 Green

Routine

Mild symptoms, follow-ups, refills, routine reviews.

Standard queue. Full intake saved.
