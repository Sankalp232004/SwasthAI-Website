Clinical Priority System

Four levels. Instant assignment.

Doctor retains full override control at all times.

🚨 Emergency

Immediate Action

Chest pain, severe breathing difficulty, altered consciousness, life-threatening acute presentation.

Flags to doctor. Seen first. No exceptions.
🔴 Red

Urgent

Severe acute pain, critical vitals, high fever with distress. Cannot safely wait behind routine cases.

Elevated in queue immediately on check-in.
🟡 Amber

Semi-Urgent

Moderate symptoms requiring timely attention. Managed within flow but above routine checkups.

Monitored for deterioration. Adjusts dynamically.
🟢 Green

Routine

Mild or non-urgent symptoms. Follow-ups, prescription refills, routine checkups and reviews.

Standard queue. Full intake recorded.
🔒 This is NOT a diagnosis. SwasthAI organises your queue. The doctor makes every clinical decision and can override at any time.
